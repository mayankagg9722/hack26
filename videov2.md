Create a cinematic 3-minute enterprise technology story video introducing an AI-powered autonomous migration agent called "Zen".

The video should NOT feel like a generic AI advertisement. It should feel like a realistic enterprise customer story that begins with a painful ITSM migration problem and gradually introduces Zen as the solution.

STYLE:
- Premium enterprise SaaS product video
- Realistic modern corporate environment
- Cinematic but professional
- Freshworks-style clean enterprise technology aesthetic
- Blue/white/neutral technology interface
- Realistic UI screens
- Smooth transitions
- Subtle background music
- Human emotions should be believable
- Avoid exaggerated futuristic robots
- Zen should feel like software/AI, not a physical robot

CHARACTERS:

Maya:
IT/customer onboarding manager.
Professional, capable, responsible for getting the customer live on Freshservice.
She initially feels confident, then increasingly overwhelmed by migration complexity, and finally relieved when Zen takes over.

Rahul:
Migration specialist/engineer.
Technical and knowledgeable but overwhelmed by spreadsheets, mappings, failures and manual troubleshooting.

Sarah:
Business stakeholder.
Focused on the go-live deadline and business confidence.
Her key question throughout the story is:
"Are we ready?"

Zen:
An intelligent AI migration agent represented through a clean enterprise UI.
Zen should communicate clearly and calmly.
Zen discovers data, creates mappings, tests migration readiness, executes migration, diagnoses failures, performs safe remediation and explains results.

"For an enterprise, moving to a new ITSM platform sounds simple. Until you realize you have to move everything that makes your business work."

Maya asks Rahul to handle the migration.
