• How Zen works

Understands. Sees.
Guides. Escalates.

Zen starts every session by understanding the customer's goals, their setup, and what they're trying to achieve. No fixed script. It builds a tailored plan for each customer based on their actual situation.

With one click the customer shares their screen. Zen reads the live UI, knows exactly which step they're on, and never gives generic instructions that don't match what's in front of them.

Zen highlights the exact button, explains why it matters, and waits. If the customer takes a wrong turn, it course-corrects in real time instead of letting them stall.

Contract questions, custom pricing, edge-case bugs. Zen recognises what it shouldn't answer, hands over to your team with the full session context attached, and keeps the customer informed.
